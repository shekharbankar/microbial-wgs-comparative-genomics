# Phase 2: Genome Selection Criteria

## Project
Comparative Genomics and Antimicrobial Resistance Gene Profiling of Escherichia coli

## Purpose
Define transparent criteria before selecting the final genome dataset.

## Selection Criteria

1. Assembly quality
   Prefer RefSeq assemblies labelled Complete Genome.
   Review assembly reports and available quality information before selection.

2. Genome length
   Compare reported genome lengths and investigate unusual values.
   Do not reject an assembly based on size alone.

3. Metadata availability
   Prefer candidates with useful strain, BioProject, sample source,
   collection date, and assembly metadata where available.

4. Genome diversity
   Avoid excessive representation of closely related isolates from one study.
   Review BioProject accessions and strain information before selection.

5. AMR relevance
   Review available resistance-related metadata and published study context.
   Do not classify an isolate as resistant or susceptible from missing metadata.

6. Data availability
   Confirm that genome FASTA files and the required annotation information
   can be obtained for the selected assemblies.

## Planned Dataset
Target: approximately 10 E. coli genomes.

## Selection Status
The final genome list has not yet been selected.

## Planned Analyses
- Genome length and GC percentage comparison
- Genome similarity analysis
- Phylogenetic analysis, where appropriate
- AMR gene detection using a suitable tool and database
- Careful interpretation of genotype and phenotype evidence

## Scientific Caution
Genome selection criteria must be applied consistently.
Detected AMR genes do not automatically establish clinical resistance.
All final selections and analysis results must be documented.
